## Description

Nash's Viking Chronicles is a free strategy game set on the real roads of Canada and the United States. Found a hall in your own town. Raise Vikings or Grogs. Send a war band down the road, or get in the car and let the map become the saga.

You do not have to drive. From the couch you can build the village, train the army, open the shop, and read the saga. From the map, send a war band, a longship, or a raven to a real town. The march takes time based on distance, and the game speaks when they leave and when they arrive. If you never share your location, search for a town and play there. Chesterville, Ontario, is the saga's home if you want it.

Driving is a bonus, not a requirement. When the phone is moving, Adventure Mode speaks the road: ambushes, beasts, treasure, rival bands, and mead halls. What you win comes home to the village. Standing in a town yourself pays more than sending a company. Voice commands work with “Hey Valhalla,” so a passenger, or a parked player, can give orders. Above a walking pace the small menus lock. That is on purpose.

Two players on the same server share one map. Invite a friend with a link. Their hall shows up, and you can siege it or raid it. Towns change hands. The battle cuts away from the map onto a field of fighters, then comes back.

The game is free. Claiming a town, recruiting, and fighting do not require a purchase. Nash Points are an optional extra. On this iPhone app they are consumable packs sold by Apple. On the website the same shelf is a placeholder and charges nothing. There is no subscription.

A recovery word in your hall lets you sign in again on another phone.

## Privacy nutrition label

Data linked to the user (the warrior name and device token, not the Apple ID):

- Location: precise location, used for the map, founding a hall, and Adventure Mode while driving. Not used for tracking. Not used for advertising.
- Audio data: microphone, used on device to hear “Hey Valhalla.” Not stored as a recording on our server.
- User ID: a random device token so the hall can be saved and restored. Not used for tracking.
- Gameplay content: name, army, towns, and optional Nash Points balance, stored on the game server so the saga continues.
- Purchases: handled by Apple. We store the transaction id so a pack is not granted twice. We do not receive a card number.

Data not collected: contacts, photos, health, browsing history, advertising identifiers for tracking, emails, physical address.

Tracking: No. We do not track users across other companies’ apps or websites.

## Review notes

The game is playable with no car and no purchase.

1. Install the build. Allow or deny location. Denying is a supported way to play.
2. Take any name. Swear to the Nash clan or the Grogs.
3. If location was denied, search “Chesterville” and found the hall there. A longhouse and a few warriors are already yours.
4. Open Village to build. Open the map, tap a nearby town, and send a war band. You do not need to move the phone. The fight, when it starts, leaves the map and plays on a field, then returns.
5. Driving is optional. A moving phone switches to Adventure Mode and speaks encounters. Please do not review this while driving.
6. The website shop at the same game is a free placeholder and charges nothing. This iOS build sells optional consumable Nash Points through StoreKit. The points appear on the hoard.
